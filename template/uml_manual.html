Manual K-Means Exercise

A fully worked, step-by-step K-Means simulation (no scikit-learn) over {{ n_records }} motorcycles, using two numerical variables.

1. Dataset and Variables

The dataset contains {{ n_records }} motorcycles described by two numerical variables, both directly related to the group's topic (grouping motorcycles by technical characteristics):

  • Engine displacement (cc): the total volume swept by the engine's pistons, in cubic centimeters. It is the standard indicator of a motorcycle's mechanical size/category, and correlates strongly with power, weight and intended use.
  • Price (COP): the motorcycle's retail price in Colombian pesos, which reflects displacement, brand positioning and included technology.

The 100 records were synthetically generated (see generate_manual_dataset.py in the repository) around three realistic market segments -- urban/commuter, mid-size, and high-performance -- so the manual simulation has genuine structure to converge to.

The overall within-cluster variance decreases (or stays essentially flat once converged) from Iteration 1 to Iteration {{ iterations|length }}, which confirms the algorithm is doing its job: each update pulls the centroids toward the true center of their group, shrinking the average squared distance between every record and its assigned centroid. By the last iteration the cluster assignments stop changing (the process has converged).

4. Final Result and Cluster Characteristics

Using the last iteration's centroids and assignments:

  • Cluster 1 (Urban): lowest displacement and lowest price -- consistent with small, economical commuter motorcycles.
  • Cluster 2 (Mid-size): intermediate displacement and price -- consistent with all-purpose, naked-style motorcycles used for both city and longer rides.
  • Cluster 3 (High-performance): highest displacement and by far the highest price -- consistent with sport/touring motorcycles.

These three manually-derived groups are consistent with the four data-driven segments found later by scikit-learn in the Clustering Application (which adds power, weight, fuel consumption and top speed, and therefore is able to split the high-performance group further into Sport and Touring/Adventure).
