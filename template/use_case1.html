Use Case 1: Disease Risk Prediction in Healthcare

A supervised learning application in medicine.

Problem Context

Hospitals collect large amounts of patient data (lab results, vital signs, medical history) but doctors cannot manually cross-check every record against known risk patterns for every patient, which can delay early diagnosis of conditions such as diabetes or heart disease.

Data That Could Be Used

Historical patient records including age, blood pressure, cholesterol levels, glucose levels, BMI, and the final diagnosis for each past patient.

What the Model Should Predict / Classify

Whether a new patient is at high, medium, or low risk of developing a specific condition, based on their current lab values and history.

Type of Machine Learning

Supervised Learning (Classification) — the model is trained on past patients whose diagnosis is already known.

Potential Benefit

Earlier detection of at-risk patients, allowing doctors to prioritize care and potentially prevent the condition from progressing.
